# flowerbyte

A personal digital space for my projects, creative work, and everything I build along the way.

## About

Flowerbyte is my personal website, created to bring together information about me, my interests, creative work, and ongoing projects in one place.

It serves as a central hub for my digital presence, connecting different areas of my work, from music and design to programming and other creative experiments.

## Sections

- **Home** — An overview of my digital presence.
- **Projects** — Personal projects and experiments.
- **About** — More information about me.
- **Portfolio** — Selected creative work.
- **Discography** — Music releases and projects.
- **Setup** — My equipment and software.
- **Home Server** — Documentation of my personal server.
- **Contact** — Ways to get in touch.
